Command Module Columbia

The Apollo 11 command module Columbia carried astronauts Neil Armstrong, Buzz Aldrin, and Michael Collins to the Moon and back on July 24, 1969, completing the mission that fulfilled a national goal set in 1961.

Object facts

  • Manufacturer: North American Rockwell, Downey, California
  • Launch vehicle: Saturn V, Kennedy Space Center, July 16, 1969
  • Weight: 11,700 pounds (5,300 kilograms)
  • Crew capacity: 3 astronauts
  • Mission duration: 8 days, 3 hours, 18 minutes
  • Splashdown: Pacific Ocean, 13.32 degrees N, 169.15 degrees W

Where to see it

Columbia is on display at the National Air and Space Museum on the National Mall in Washington, D.C., in the Boeing Milestones of Flight Hall. The module traveled to all 50 states and the District of Columbia on a state-by-state tour from 2019 to 2022 before returning to display.
