Hurricane Season Outlook — National Weather Service

The Atlantic hurricane season runs June 1 to November 30. NOAA's Climate Prediction Center issues seasonal outlooks each May and August, and the National Hurricane Center tracks storms from formation to landfall.

Saffir-Simpson scale

  • Category 1: winds 74 to 95 mph
  • Category 2: winds 96 to 110 mph
  • Category 3: winds 111 to 129 mph — major hurricane
  • Category 4: winds 130 to 156 mph — major hurricane
  • Category 5: winds 157 mph and higher

Prepare by June

Know your evacuation zone, keep 7 days of water and supplies per person, and sign up for local alerts. Forecast cones show the probable path of the storm center, not its size.
