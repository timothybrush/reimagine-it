Artemis II — Mission Overview

Artemis II is the first crewed flight test of NASA's Space Launch System rocket and the Orion spacecraft. Four astronauts will travel around the Moon on an approximately 10-day mission — the farthest humans have traveled beyond the far side of the Moon.

Flight plan

  • Launch: SLS Block 1 from Kennedy Space Center, pad 39B
  • Crew of 4 on Orion
  • Free-return trajectory around the Moon
  • Distance: about 230,000 km from Earth
  • Splashdown: Pacific Ocean

Program record so far

The uncrewed Artemis I test flight flew in 2022 and spent 25.5 days in space, reaching 43,892 km beyond the Moon before Orion returned.
