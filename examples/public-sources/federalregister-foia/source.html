Freedom of Information Act Regulations; Final Rule

Each agency is adopting final amendments to its Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) regulations to implement the FOIA Improvement Act of 2016. The rule was signed June 22, 2026, and takes effect August 1, 2026.

Dates and comments

  • Comments must be received on or before July 7, 2026
  • Effective date: August 1, 2026
  • Public inspection: July 2, 2026, 8:45 a.m.

Procedural requirements

Executive Order 12866 (regulatory planning and review) does not apply because this rule is procedural. The Regulatory Flexibility Act does not apply to an agency rule of procedure and practice. The Congressional Review Act provisions at 5 U.S.C. 804(2) exempt procedural rules from the 60-day delayed effective date.

How to respond

Written comments may be submitted by mail to the Office of Information Policy, or electronically at the federal rulemaking portal regulations.gov. For further information contact the FOIA Public Liaison at doi_foia@doi.example. Source facts from the Federal Register (public domain).
