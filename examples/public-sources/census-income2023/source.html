Income in the United States: 2023

Real median household income in 2023 was $80,610, not statistically different from the 2022 estimate of $77,540. Official poverty rate in 2023 was 11.1 percent, down 0.5 percentage points from 2022.

Household income

  • Median household income: $80,610 (2023 dollars)
  • Median earnings for full-time, year-round men: $66,790
  • Median earnings for full-time, year-round women: $55,240
  • The 2023 female-to-male earnings ratio: 0.826
  • Real median income for family households: $102,800
  • Real median income for nonfamily households: $49,890

Poverty

In 2023, 37.1 million people were in poverty, a decrease of 1.5 million from 2022. The poverty rate for children under 18 declined 1.4 percentage points, from 12.5 percent to 11.2 percent. The supplemental poverty measure was 12.9 percent in 2023, up from 12.4 percent in 2022.
