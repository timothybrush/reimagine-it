Yellowstone National Park — Season Visitor Bulletin

Established March 1, 1872, Yellowstone was the world's first national park. The park covers 3,471 square miles — 2,221,766 acres — with 96 percent of the park in Wyoming, 3 percent in Montana, and 1 percent in Idaho. Five entrances serve the park: North, Northeast, South, East, and West.

Entrance passes

  • Private, non-commercial vehicle: $35 — valid 7 days
  • Motorcycle: $30 — valid 7 days
  • Individual (on foot or bicycle): $20 — valid 7 days
  • Annual park pass: $70

Old Faithful

The famous geyser erupts every 60 to 110 minutes; eruptions last 1.5 to 5 minutes. Rangers post predicted eruption times at the visitor education center each morning.

Plan ahead

Cell coverage is limited across the interior. Download maps before you arrive, carry layers for sudden weather changes, and stay at least 25 yards from bison and elk at all times.
